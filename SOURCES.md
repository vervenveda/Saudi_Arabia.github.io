Updated: 2026-10-24

This register supports the public Saudi Arabia country gateway. It records the cultural design references and regional carousel photographs used by the page. A photo credit on the page is not a substitute for this source record.

## Cultural design references

- **At-Turaif / Najdi architecture** — UNESCO World Heritage Centre, *At-Turaif District in ad-Dir'iyah*. UNESCO describes the property as an outstanding example of Najdi architectural and decorative style, including adobe construction and geometric decoration adapted to central Arabia's desert environment.  
  https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1329

- **Historic Jeddah / Roshan architecture** — UNESCO World Heritage Centre, *Historic Jeddah, the Gate to Makkah*. The record describes coral-stone building traditions and large wooden Roshan façades associated with Red Sea trade and pilgrimage.  
  https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1361

- **Al-Qatt Al-Asiri** — UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage, *Al-Qatt Al-Asiri, female traditional interior wall decoration in Asir*.  
  https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/al-qatt-al-asiri-female-traditional-interior-wall-decoration-in-asir-saudi-arabia-01261

- **Al Sadu** — UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage, *Traditional weaving of Al Sadu*. UNESCO describes rhythmic geometric repetition, symmetry and bright colors including reds and oranges.  
  https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/traditional-weaving-of-al-sadu-02158

## Image-use rule

Every new country-site image should record:
- exact subject/place;
- photographer or archive;
- capture/publication date where known;
- source URL;
- reuse license or public-domain basis;
- whether the view is documentary, archival, illustrative or reconstructed.

Illustrations must never be presented as documentary photographs.

## Language rule

The Saudi gateway is intended to maintain full English ↔ Arabic parity, including visible copy, dynamic regional content, controls, accessibility labels and saved language preference.
