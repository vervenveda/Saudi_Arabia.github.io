02 / A CULTURAL CONSTELLATION

Not one aesthetic, but many

Saudi Arabia’s cultural forms belong to different regions, communities, materials and historical moments. The portals are designed to make those differences visible rather than collapsing them into a single national decoration.

Research basis

This cultural hall draws first from UNESCO World Heritage and Intangible Cultural Heritage records, then uses documented image sources already registered in the Saudi gateway.
