01 / REGIONAL PERFORMANCE

Different traditions, different social spaces

NAJD

Alardah Alnajdiyah

UNESCO describes a performance combining dance, drumming and chanted poetry, traditionally marking major occasions and built around poets, rows of performers and percussion.

CENTRAL & NORTHERN VARIATIONS

Samri

Samri includes regional variants and commonly brings sung poetry, clapping, drum rhythm and coordinated movement into evening social performance.

HIJAZ

Almezmar

A Hijazi group performance using two facing rows, drumming, clapping, chant and sticks; UNESCO records it as living intangible heritage.

RED SEA

Maritime song & Semsemiah

Sea-connected communities carry their own musical worlds. UNESCO notes Semsemiah playing among Saudi sailors, fishermen and ship-trading communities, closely associated with the sea.

20TH CENTURY

Radio, recording and the Saudi song

Modern Saudi song developed through local practice, recordings, radio ensembles, cassette culture and artists working across wider Arab musical networks.

TODAY

Education, venues and new scenes

Contemporary Saudi musical life includes heritage ensembles, popular song, independent musicians, education initiatives, festivals and expanding public performance.
