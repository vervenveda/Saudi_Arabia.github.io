01 / VISUAL TIMELINE

From rock face to living room wall

UP TO 10,000 YEARS OF ROCK-ART HISTORY

Hail · Jubbah and Shuwaymis

UNESCO describes dense petroglyph and inscription landscapes recording human and animal figures across major environmental changes.

NABATAEAN HEGRA

Carved monument and inscription

At Hegra, monumental rock-cut tomb façades and inscriptions place northwestern Arabia inside wider networks of craft, exchange and Nabataean culture.

LIVING HERITAGE

Al-Qatt Al-Asiri

Traditionally practiced by women in Asir, Al-Qatt uses white gypsum grounds and vivid geometric shapes and symbols, transmitted through observation and practice.

TEXTILE GEOMETRY

Al-Sadu

Bedouin women weave durable textiles from natural fibres; UNESCO notes rhythmic repetition, symmetry and bright colors including reds and oranges.

LETTER AS FORM

Arabic calligraphy

Calligraphy connects language, manuscript culture, architecture, design and contemporary practice; UNESCO recognizes Arabic calligraphy as shared intangible heritage across multiple countries including Saudi Arabia.
