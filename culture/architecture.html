01 / REGIONAL ARCHITECTURES

Four environments, four design vocabularies

NAJD

Adobe, enclosure & geometry

At-Turaif demonstrates a Najdi architectural language using adobe, compact urban form and geometric decoration adapted to central Arabia’s environment.

HIJAZ

Coral stone & roshan

Historic Jeddah combines coral masonry with large wooden roshan that filter light, support ventilation and give tower houses their distinctive facades.

ASIR

Mountain building & color

Asir’s highland environments support distinctive stone-and-earth building traditions, while interior visual culture includes Al-Qatt Al-Asiri.

AL-AHSA

Oasis as infrastructure

Al-Ahsa shows architecture as landscape: springs, canals, agriculture, paths and settlements form an evolving cultural system rather than a single monument.
