# Saudi Arabia — Primary and Near-Primary Source Directory

This directory points learners toward original records, item-level heritage evidence and archival collections. A directory link is a research starting point, not an endorsement of every interpretation.

## Archaeology and inscriptions

- **UNESCO World Heritage Centre — Rock Art in the Hail Region**  
  https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1472  
  Use for property documentation and UNESCO's synthesis; follow nomination/bibliography links for deeper archaeological work.

- **UNESCO World Heritage Centre — Hegra Archaeological Site**  
  https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1293  
  Use for documented tombs, inscriptions, wells and property history.

- **UNESCO World Heritage Centre — At-Turaif District in ad-Dir'iyah**  
  https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1329

- **UNESCO World Heritage Centre — Historic Jeddah, the Gate to Makkah**  
  https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1361

- **UNESCO World Heritage Centre — Al-Ahsa Oasis**  
  https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1563

## Historical and archival research

- **Library of Congress — Saudi Arabia: A Country Study (1993)**  
  https://www.loc.gov/item/93028506/  
  A dated Federal Research Division synthesis with bibliography. Useful for historiography and twentieth-century background; do not treat its 1992 research cutoff as current information.

- **Saudi Founding Day official guide**  
  https://foundingday.sa/  
  Primary/official evidence for the modern state's Founding Day chronology and commemorative framing.

- **Saudi Ministry of Foreign Affairs — KSA History**  
  https://www.mofa.gov.sa/en/ksa/Pages/history.aspx  
  Official state chronology. Use as evidence of official historical framing and corroborate broader interpretations independently.

## Newspapers and historical state records

Look for item-level copies or catalogue records of **Umm Al-Qura**, the official Saudi newspaper, when researching royal decrees and state announcements. Cite the specific issue/date rather than citing a secondary summary if the primary item is available.

## Image archives

The repository's `SOURCES.md` records each documentary image currently used on the public gateway, including creator, source and license. Reuse the item-level record rather than copying an unverified image from search results.
