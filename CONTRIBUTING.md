# Contributing to the Saudi Arabia Research Archive

This repository welcomes careful corrections, source additions and research expansion.

## Before proposing a change

1. Identify the exact claim, page or data record.
2. Provide an item-level or high-quality source whenever possible.
3. Explain what the source directly establishes.
4. State any important limitation, conflict of interest or uncertainty.
5. For current claims, include the source date and reporting period.
6. For images, include creator/archive and reuse rights.

## Historical care

Do not:
- retroactively label prehistoric, Nabataean, early Islamic or other earlier societies as the modern Saudi state;
- erase regional differences among Najd, Hijaz, Asir, the Eastern Province, Hail, the northwest or other communities;
- treat official national narratives as the only possible historiography;
- treat hostile or oppositional claims as true merely because they oppose an official narrative.

## Living culture

Name a practice carefully, identify where and by whom it is documented, and distinguish a living tradition from a modern decorative adaptation.

## Corrections

Corrections should explain what changed and why. When evidence is genuinely mixed, prefer labels such as **contested**, **provisional** or **source-specific** over false certainty.
