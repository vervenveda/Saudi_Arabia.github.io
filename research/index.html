02 / RESEARCH SAFEGUARDS

A source is evidence for something specific

Primary evidence

Artifacts, inscriptions, documents, photographs, maps and contemporaneous records can establish particular observations; they do not automatically explain themselves.

Institutional sources

UNESCO can establish its heritage findings; a Saudi government source can establish an official position or record. Neither is automatically independent corroboration for every broader claim.

Historical labels

Do not call Nabataean Hegra or prehistoric Hail the modern Saudi state. Name the historical society, period and geography accurately.

Current claims

Date statistics, development promises and policy claims. Compare announced goals with measured outcomes and independent evidence.
