# Saudi_Arabia.github.io

Verve N Veda’s bilingual Saudi Arabia country gateway: an independent educational project focused on regions, heritage, cultural life, history, research pathways, and international context.

## Public gateways

- `index.html` — main Saudi Arabia gateway, EN ↔ AR
- `arabic-companion/index.html` — Arabic language and hospitality learning companion
- `culture/index.html` — Saudi Cultural Hall
- `culture/art.html` — Art & Pattern: rock art, Hegra, Al-Qatt Al-Asiri, Al-Sadu, calligraphy
- `culture/music.html` — Music & Performance: Ardah, Samri, Almezmar, maritime traditions, Semsemiah, modern music
- `culture/history.html` — History Through the Ages
- `culture/architecture.html` — Architecture & Design: Najd, Hijaz, Asir, Al-Ahsa

## Shared cultural portal infrastructure

- `assets/css/saudi-culture.css` — shared layout and accessibility standards with subject-specific visual treatments
- `assets/js/saudi-culture.js` — persistent English ↔ Arabic switching, RTL support, accessible tab interactions, pattern study and rhythm study controls
- `SOURCES.md` — research, image, license, and interpretive register

## Editorial principles

1. Shared functional standards do not mean a copied visual identity.
2. Regional traditions are named by region and community wherever possible.
3. Earlier societies in the geography of present-day Saudi Arabia are not retroactively described as the modern Saudi state.
4. UNESCO and item-level documentary sources anchor heritage claims; governmental or state-affiliated sources are identified as such.
5. Interactive pattern and rhythm studies are original educational abstractions, never presented as authentic reproductions or musical transcriptions.
6. Documentary images require source and reuse records.
7. The public gateway remains an independent educational project, not a Saudi government website.

## Language standard

The main gateway and cultural portals maintain English ↔ Arabic parity with right-to-left layout support and a saved language preference.

## Development

The site is static HTML/CSS/JavaScript and requires no build step. Work is maintained on `main`.
